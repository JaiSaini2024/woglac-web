Privacy & Data Policy

Last updated: October 2024

Worship On Guitar - LAC ("WOGLAC") is committed to protecting the privacy of musicians, worship leaders, and users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when using our application.

1. Information We Collect

WOGLAC operates primarily offline and locally on your device. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

  • Account Information: If you sign up/in, we collect basic authentication data (such as email address and user ID) to sync setlists and personal preferences.
  • Local Storage: Song bookmarks, custom chords, transposition preferences, and setlists are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services like Firebase (for authentication, analytics, and database sync) which may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical security measures to protect user data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official support channels.
